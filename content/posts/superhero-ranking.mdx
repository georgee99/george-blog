Just a quick list of my favourite superheroes and why I think they're the best

| Rank | Superhero | Notes |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Spiderman | By far the best in my opinion. He perfectly balances humour, humanity, and perseverance, which is why he's my favourite superhero |
| 2 | Superman | He's the best representation of hope in comic books and is the epitomy of kindness |
| 3 | Michaelangelo | He's the most cheerful of the four and has the biggest heart, and IMO has the best weapons |
| 4 | Daredevil | Matt Murdock is an incredibly relatable, flawed, and deeply human superhero. He doesn't have a healing factor but still gets up after getting beaten |
| 5 | Captain America | Cap is one of the best leaders in comics because he always stands by his principles, even when it's difficult |
| 6 | Nightwing | He's the perfect balance between Batman and Superman. He fights like Batman but has more humanity |
| 7 | Black Widow | Natasha is highly skilled to the point where she survives alongside super-soldiers. Her determination makes her very likeable |
| 8 | Black Panther | T'Challa's nobility and compassion never disappear, even when he's forced into impossible situations |
| 9 | Leonardo | He's the backbone of the group. He has great leadership skills and is the most disciplined and responsible |
| 10 | Batman | Arguably the most skilled and intelligent superhero in the DCU. Even without powers he is one of the most feared  |
