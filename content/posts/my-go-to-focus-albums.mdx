Just a ranked list of my favourite music albums when I need to focus. Not just limited to coding, 
but even for things like chess, writing, and research. 

### 1. [Ágætis byrjun - Sigur Rós](https://open.spotify.com/album/1DMMv1Kmoli3Y9fVEZDUVC)

It has a great calming effect. I used it for studying math back in university. The vocals feel like an 
additional instrument and it weirdly disconnects you from reality. It's perfect to remove any distractions.

### 2. [Ascend - Illenium](https://open.spotify.com/album/60xcVwuQJAOyu11xf9mObS)

Melodic EDM is one of my favourite genres of music. Illenium's collabs always have soothing and tuneful 
vocals. It pairs very well with his music style. There's a lot of emotional build-ups and cinematic drops 
which keep the energy going and helps you avoid mental exhaustion.

### 3. [American Dream - LCD Soundsystem](https://open.spotify.com/album/0hdimlCTCms7otJCX9OvqM)

This fits into the Synth-pop genre, which a lot of coders love due to its futuristic and ultramodern theme.
Listening makes you feel like you're in a sci-fi movie, which helps you get into the flow state you need 
for deep focus.

### 4. [Wide-Eyed - Said The Sky](https://open.spotify.com/album/6GnQAEXPs7bGDJCeshofZY)

Said The Sky is up there with one of the best producers of Melodic EDM. It's a bit more expressive than 
other artists and gives a good physical feeling. It helps especially with tedious tasks.

### 5. [Valtari - Sigur Rós](https://open.spotify.com/album/0ZHkshyLAi9f8DXdj3Z5ph)

Valtari has a very ambient and mellow tempo. Each track contains incredible Icelandic vocals which compliments
the music and gives it a lot of texture. I found this album through the standout track "Varúð".
