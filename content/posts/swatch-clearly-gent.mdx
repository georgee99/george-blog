The Swatch Clearly Gent is definitely a standout piece. I bought this with the goal of finding a unique everyday watch. It's marketed as a watch which can start a conversation, and they're not wrong at all. I definitely rate this as one of my favourite pieces due to look and monetary value.
It's easy to throw and, and it makes watches feel less serious.  

---

### First Impressions

I saw this watch while scrolling through the Swatch website. The goal was to find something fun, affordable, and visually different from the usual everyday watches. 
Swatch offers a variety of watches which fit this description, which made my choice more difficult. 
The main reason I chose the Clearly Gent was the clear and see-through design. When I tried it on in person, I knew it was the best choice Swatch offered. 

The transparent design along with the coloured hands gave the watch some presence. One thing I noticed after trying it on was the sound of the ticking. Due to the bio-sourced case, the sound was louder. However it was only noticeable when it was right up against your ear.

---

### Design & Build

The design was the best part about the watch. It's simple, but every element of it added to its appearance. 

One of the drawbacks was the band. It's made from plastic which can feel weird after using leather for so long. A switch to a silicone translucent one is quite common for this style though. Also, there aren't any numbers on the watch which makes readability a bit difficult. 

Overall though it looks solid, so you don't mind putting up with a little bit of a hassle.

---

### Daily Wear Experience

Depending on your lifestyle, this can be a great daily watch. Because I work in the office most days, I usually opt for my Hamilton instead. I usually wear this on weekends though since it fits better with casual wear.

---

### Verdict

Overall, I'd definitely recommend this watch. For the low price, there's really no risk. It reminds you that watches don't 
need to be expensive.

It's simple but stands out, which is what a lot of people want in an everyday watch. 

If you're looking for something which enhances your style, this is definitely a good option.
