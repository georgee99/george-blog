FYI, this is the US Version. I've rewatched this sitcom way too many times to count. Narrowing it down to just 10 wasn't easy. I'm basing this list on how much I enjoyed watching them. 


### 10. Casino Night

One of the most important episodes in the entire series. The casino games were entertaining, but the episode is remembered for the final few minutes which completely changed the direction of Jim and Pam's arc.

---

### 9. The Deposition

This episode showed Michael at his most vulnerable while also delivering some of the funniest corporate drama in the series. The back-and-forth between Michael, Jan, and David Wallace was done brilliantly. 

---

### 8. The Job

This was a great season finale and it was exciting to see what would happen with Jim, Pam, and Karen. This episode had lots of romance, havoc, and suspense. While it answered a lot of questions we also had some cliffhangers which balanced this out.

---

### 7. Goodbye Toby

Although it was an hour long, you'd be focused the whole time. There were heaps of moments to reel you in and make you laugh. It also introduced Holly who made an immediate impression.

---

### 6. Gay Witch Hunt

This was a messed up episode, but that's what makes it one of the better ones and fun to watch. It was also good to see a follow up of what happened on casino night, and where Jim and Pam's relationship went. 

---

### 5. Scotts Tot's

Some would say this is the worst episode of The Office, and I wouldn't blame them. It's honestly impossible to finish without flinching or looking away.

---

### 4. Niagara: Part 2

Jim and Pam getting married was the best finish to one of the longest slow burns in sitcom history. The wedding was chaotic, funny, and very satisfying.

---

### 3. Dinner Party

Definitely one of the most awkward episodes out there but you can't skip it. There were lots of little bits I missed on the first watch. Every character felt trapped and uncomfortable which was hilarious.

---

### 2. The Injury

This one had a lot of unspoken humour. Just the way everything ties to each other makes me laugh. Michael trying to make himself the center of attention was too good.

---

### 1. Stress Relief

The chaotic fire drill cold open alone springs this to the top of the list. The rest of the episode has memes that still hold till this day. The roast and CPR training gave the whole cast their moment to shine and make us laugh. There were also soft moments too which balanced it. 

---

### Honourable Mentions

- The Dundies
- Fun Run
- Threat Level Midnight
- Diwali
