I've watched a lot of TV shows over the years, but these are the ones that have stuck with me the most. This ranking is based purely on how much I enjoyed each one.
| Rank | TV Show | Notes |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Daredevil | Best superhero show ever made. Great choreography, characters, and storytelling. |
| 2 | Breaking Bad | One of the greatest character arcs in television. Every season somehow gets better than the last. |
| 3 | Better Call Saul | The payoff in the last season is worth it. Arguably the best written prequel ever made. |
| 4 | The Office (US) | Endless rewatch value. One of the funniest workplace comedies ever made. |
| 5 | Hannibal | Visually stunning psychological thriller. Mads Mikkelsen's carried the series, but the support cast were also great. |
| 6 | How I Met Your Mother | Comfort show with memorable characters. The ending was mid, but the journey was worth it. |
| 7 | Seinfeld | Timeless comedy that influenced almost every sitcom that followed. Surprisingly well aged. |
| 8 | Dexter | One of the most unique TV concepts. Strong early seasons despite the controversial ending. |
| 9 | The Sopranos | You can definitely see why so many people call this one of the best TV shows ever made. It lives up to the hype. |
| 10 | Planet Earth | The best nature documentary series ever produced. The cinematography is unmatched. |
